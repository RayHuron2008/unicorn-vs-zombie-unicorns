WHAT CHANGED
- Falling presents are gone.
- Defeating a gun zombie grants its gun to the player who killed it, using the
  same five-second duration as your current game's normal weapon mechanic.
  Zombies crushed by the statue do not award you a gun.
- A small toy rocket and toy fire engine sit in the two narrow stock aisles
  behind the main shop floor. Walk up an aisle and approach its toy to discover
  a missile jetpack or flamethrower. Ordinary fighting stays below these aisles.
- Each player can discover each hidden item once per level attempt. The hidden
  powers last 12 seconds. The jetpack carries six homing missiles. Hold B to fire.
  A stolen gun does not interrupt hidden equipment that is already active.
- The jetpack flies above ground stomps and chomps, but grabbing hands can
  still reach you. Losing a life removes your equipped powers.

THE NEW BOSS FIGHT
1. LEGS: Attack either leg while avoiding his marked stomps. Each leg breaks
   separately. Once both are destroyed, his body drops to the floor.
2. ARMS: His fallen body swivels and slithers toward you. His remaining hands
   try to grab you. Destroy both arms. A successful grab hurts and briefly holds
   you, then releases; a shield blocks the grab and breaks instead.
3. HEAD: With no limbs left, he lunges and chomps along his aimed path. Dodge
   three chomps. He then becomes exhausted, and his head becomes vulnerable.
   Attack the head during that pause. If he survives, the sequence repeats.

Use A to headbutt and the existing dodge controls to evade. B fires your weapon.
The fight can be completed without either secret item. Weapons can damage the
currently attackable part, but they cannot bypass the head's exhausted opening.
The one-minute main battle and the statue's five-second step remain. Any zombies
still alive after the timer must be defeated before the boss wakes up.
