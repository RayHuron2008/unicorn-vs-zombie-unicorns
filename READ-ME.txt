HOW THE LEVEL WORKS
- Fight the usual zombie unicorns for one minute, then finish any still alive.
- The central nutcracker steps forward every five seconds. The marked stepping
  path warns you first, and it crushes zombies as well as the player.
- An unprotected stomp or fist impact costs one life. A shield absorbs the hit.
- After the main battle, the nutcracker wakes, follows you, tries three stomps,
  then aims a fist slam. Watch the warning and move out before the impact.
- Its grounded fist rests for one second, then its arm lifts slowly. Approach
  the glowing hand during that first second and move toward its face to climb.
  At the face, press A or B to land the hit. The unicorn retreats safely afterward.
- Three successful face hits beat all three rounds. Later rounds move faster,
  aim sooner, and give you less time on the arm. Missing an opening repeats it.
- Falling presents open when you approach them. Some contain ordinary toys;
  others grant fire breath for 5 seconds, a laser for 8 seconds, or an 8-second
  jetpack with six homing missiles. Hold B to use a power.
- The jetpack hovers over stomps, but the fist can still hit you. Weapons can
  stagger the lowered arm once per opening, adding a little climbing time.
  Reaching and striking the face is still required to defeat each round.
- Both multiplayer players share the same nutcracker, presents, and victory.
